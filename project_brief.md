# My agent: GrowthMarketer AI

One-liner: A conversational marketing consultant agent that helps entrepreneurs and product creators formulate content strategies, optimize listing prices, and evaluate marketing channel plans with lead-and-pricing metrics.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers business profile, target audience demographic, brand voice, budget constraints, and historical campaign results across sessions.
- Tools: Looks up channel performance benchmarks, checks lead acquisition estimates, generates content calendars, and retrieves pricing models.
- Catalog/UI: Catalog of marketing channels (Paid Social, SEO, Email Marketing, Influencer, Cold Outreach) rendered as structured performance cards and metrics comparison tables.
- Image gen: Generates ad visual concepts, promotional campaign banners, and social post mockups.
- Sandbox: Calculates Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), lead conversion yields, and pricing sensitivity formulas in Python.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch: Code sandbox for CAC/ROAS calculations & pricing sensitivity modeling.
